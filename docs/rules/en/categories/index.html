FITASC categories: who is a Junior, Lady, Senior, Veteran, Master

The age categories are the same for Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting, valid from 01.01.2027. Only the year of birth counts, not the day or month. National and club events may use their own categories — check the competition programme.

Categories and ages

CategoryAgeRule
Junior13–20Boys and girls from 1 January of the year they turn 13, whatever their birthday (unless a derogation is granted), until the end of the year they turn 20. In 2027: born 1 January 2007 to 31 December 2014.
Lady (Dame)13+Women and girls from 1 January of the year they turn 13 (unless a derogation is granted). The table sets no upper age limit. In 2027: born on or before 31 December 2014.
Men (Homme)21–55From 1 January of the year of the 21st birthday until the end of the year of the 55th. This is the main adult category. In 2027: born 1 January 1972 to 31 December 2006.
Senior56–65From 1 January of the year of the 56th birthday until the end of the year of the 65th. Senior is not the main adult category but the 56–65 age group. In 2027: born 1962 to 1971.
Veteran66–72From 1 January of the year of the 66th birthday until the end of the year of the 72nd. In 2027: born 1955 to 1961.
Master73+Men and women from 1 January of the year of the 73rd birthday. In 2027: born before 1 January 1955.

  • The category depends on the year of birth, not the date: a new category applies from 1 January of the year in which the shooter reaches the age.
  • The table applies from 1 January 2027. The birth-year limits are given for the 2027 season and move on by one year each year. For competitions before 1 January 2027, check the table in force at the time on the FITASC website.
  • These are international FITASC rules. National federations, clubs and organisers of national events may use their own categories and age limits; the competition programme states them.
  • A shooter selected for a team of another category (for example a junior in the Men team or a junior girl in the Lady team) must also enter that category individually.
  • In FITASC Senior means 56–65, not the main adult category; the main adult category is Men (Homme). Older results and some national programmes may use other names, such as Super Veteran.
