Warnings

Chapter 12 of the FITASC Sporting™ rulebook in our own words: every rule point with its reference and a link to the official PDF page.

Source: Official FITASC PDF ↗ · Edition: 01/01/2025 · Updated: 2026-10-09

12.1in the referees' quick guidePDF, p. 17 ↗

Yellow card for gun position

If a shooter does not take the ready position required by 3.1, or mounts the gun before the target appears, the referee gives a verbal warning backed by a yellow card.

  • Improper ready position (3.1)
  • Gun mounted before the target appears
  • Verbal warning + yellow card
Terms: Yellow card Warning Ready position
12.2in the referees' quick guidePDF, p. 17 ↗

Red card on repeat at the same layout

After the first warning, any further offence on the same layout gets a red card and the target is scored: zero on a single; zero and No Bird on a double on report; zero and zero on a simultaneous or rafale double.

  • Applies to further offences on the same layout
  • Single: zero
  • Double on report: zero + No Bird
  • Simultaneous / rafale: zero + zero
Terms: Red card Zero No Bird
12.3in the referees' quick guidePDF, p. 17 ↗

Shooting at a declared No Bird

When the referee has called No Bird, that target is off limits: it may not be shot at, whatever the reason. A shooter who still does it after a first warning gets the same scores as in 12.2: a single is zero; a double on report is zero and No Bird; a simultaneous or rafale double is zero and zero.

  • A declared No Bird may not be shot at
  • First time: warning
  • After the warning: single zero; report zero + No Bird; simultaneous/rafale zero + zero
Terms: No Bird Warning Zero

Common questions on this chapter

What happens if I mount the gun too early?

The first time you get a verbal warning with a yellow card. Any further offence on the same layout brings a red card: zero on a single, zero and No Bird on a double on report, zero and zero on simultaneous or rafale doubles.

How much time do I have between targets?

At most fifteen seconds between singles or between doubles. Exceeding it gives a warning first, then the penalties of 12.2.

Can I shoot at a target the referee has already called No Bird?

No, never. After a first warning, doing so is penalised: zero on a single, zero and No Bird on a report pair, zero and zero on simultaneous or rafale.
