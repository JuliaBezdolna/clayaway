Shoot-offs

Chapter 14 of the FITASC Compak® Sporting rulebook in our own words: every rule point with its reference and a link to the official PDF page.

Source: Official FITASC PDF ↗ · Edition: 01/01/2025 · Updated: 2026-10-09

Ch. 14PDF, p. 14 ↗

Where shoot-offs are shot

The Technical Commission members present choose the Compak and the layouts for shoot-offs; if none is present, the jury does. Shoot-offs are shot on a Compak with one single and two simultaneous doubles per station.

  • Chosen by the Technical Commission, otherwise the jury.
  • Format: 1 single + 2 simultaneous doubles per station.

Shoot-offs for the top three: 25 targets, then sudden death

A tie for a top-three place, in the open ranking or in a category, is settled by shooting 25 more targets. If the shooters are still level, they go on to sudden death: the first zero is out, and everyone shoots the same number of targets from the same stand (Annex 5). If shooters in an open-ranking shoot-off are in the same category, its result also decides their category places. Free places in a shoot-off squad stay empty.

  • Top 3 (open and categories): 25 more targets.
  • Still level: sudden death, the first zero is out.
  • Same number of targets for all, same stand.
  • Open shoot-off also settles a shared category.
  • Free squad places stay empty.

Staying available for a shoot-off

If no time is fixed in advance for the shoot-off, the shooters concerned must stay in contact with the jury and be ready to shoot less than 15 minutes after being called. A shooter absent when the shoot-off starts is treated as having withdrawn. For exceptional reasons the jury may postpone the shoot-off to the next day; absentees are then also considered withdrawn.

  • Ready in less than 15 minutes after the call.
  • Absent = withdrawn.
  • Jury may postpone to the next day.

Ties below third place: countback

Below third place in the individual ranking there is no shoot-off: tied shooters are ranked equal and listed by their scores counted back from the 8th Compak to the 1st.

  • No shoot-off below 3rd place.
  • Ranked equal, listed by countback from Compak 8 to Compak 1.
Ch. 14PDF, p. 14 ↗

Ties between national teams

Tied national teams are separated by the combined score of their members on the 8th Compak; if still level, on the 7th, then the 6th and so on back to the 1st.

  • Team totals on Compak 8 first.
  • Then Compak 7, 6, ... 1.

Sudden death with two shooters

The sudden-death Compak uses a table of one single and two simultaneous doubles. After a draw, shooter 1 and then shooter 2 shoot the single on station 1. If equal, shooter 2 shoots the first double first, then shooter 1; if still equal, shooter 1 shoots the second double, then shooter 2. They then move to station 2, where the starting order keeps alternating, and so on until the first eliminating zero with an equal number of targets shot.

  • Order fixed by drawing lots.
  • Single, then double 1, then double 2 at each station.
  • The starting shooter alternates at each step.
  • Ends at the first eliminating zero after equal targets.

Sudden death with three shooters

With three shooters the same scheme applies after a draw: all three shoot the station 1 single in order 1-2-3, the first double in order 2-3-1, the second double in order 3-1-2, then on station 2 the single in order 2-3-1, and so on in rotation. It continues until the first eliminating zero, always with an equal number of targets shot.

  • Station 1: single 1-2-3, double 1: 2-3-1, double 2: 3-1-2.
  • Station 2 single starts with shooter 2 (2-3-1).
  • Rotation continues until the first eliminating zero.

Common questions on this chapter

How is a tie for a medal decided?

By a 25-target shoot-off on a Compak with one single and two simultaneous doubles. If still tied, sudden death follows: the first zero eliminates, with equal targets shot. Be ready within 15 minutes of the call or you are considered withdrawn.

How does a sudden-death shoot-off between two shooters run?

After a draw, both shoot the station 1 single, then the first double and the second double, the starting shooter alternating each time; then they move on to station 2 and so on, until the first eliminating zero.

I tied for 5th place. Will there be a shoot-off?

No. Below third place tied shooters are ranked equal and listed by countback from the 8th Compak to the 1st.
