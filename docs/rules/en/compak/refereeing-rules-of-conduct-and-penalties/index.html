Refereeing, rules of conduct and penalties

Chapter 21 of the FITASC Compak® Sporting rulebook in our own words: every rule point with its reference and a link to the official PDF page.

Source: Official FITASC PDF ↗ · Edition: 01/01/2025 · Updated: 2026-10-09

21.1PDF, p. 24 ↗

10 seconds to call: warning, then penalty

A shooter has at most 10 seconds to call for the target after the previous station's target has been shot. A shooter who exceeds this first receives a warning noted on the score sheet; repeated in the same round, the targets are scored as in the 21.5 table: single ZERO, double on report ZERO / NO BIRD, simultaneous double ZERO / ZERO.

  • Max 10 seconds to call.
  • 1st time: warning noted on the score sheet.
  • Again in the same round: penalty per 21.5.

21.2in the referees' quick guidePDF, p. 24 ↗

Refusing a target

A shooter may not refuse a target unless he did not call for it. Refusing to shoot at a target the referee judges regular is penalised per 21.5. The quick guide spells it out: single ZERO; first target of a report double ZERO, and the double is repeated to score the second target; first target of a simultaneous double not fired at: ZERO-ZERO.

  • Only an uncalled target may be refused.
  • Single refused: ZERO.
  • Report double, 1st refused: ZERO, double repeated for the 2nd.
  • Simultaneous double refused: ZERO-ZERO.

21.3PDF, p. 25 ↗

Never shoot at a declared NO BIRD

Once the referee has clearly called NO BIRD, that target must not be shot at under any pretext. A shooter who does so is penalised per 21.5.

  • Clearly announced NO BIRD: do not shoot.
  • Penalty per 21.5.
21.4PDF, p. 25 ↗

Malfunction: inspection, 2 minutes, replacement gun

After a malfunction the shooter is penalised per 21.5 if it is not the first incident, or if he opens the gun or touches the safety before the referee has inspected it. He then has two minutes to repair or replace the gun. With the referee's agreement he may carry on with his group if he obtains another gun; otherwise the targets not shot are scored zero.

  • Do not open the gun or touch the safety before inspection.
  • Penalty if not the first incident.
  • 2 minutes to repair or replace.
  • No replacement: remaining targets = zero.
21.5PDF, p. 25 ↗

Penalty table after a first warning

After the first warning, any further breach on the same Compak brings a red card and the targets are scored by the table. For firing time (21.1), refusal (21.2) and shooting a NO BIRD (21.3): single ZERO, double on report ZERO / NO BIRD, simultaneous double ZERO / ZERO. For a malfunction (21.4): on a single ZERO; on the first target of a double, ZERO / NO BIRD (report) or ZERO / ZERO (simultaneous); on the second target of a double, the first result is kept and the second is ZERO.

  • First breach: warning; next on the same Compak: red card.
  • 21.1-21.3: single ZERO; report ZERO / NO BIRD; simultaneous ZERO / ZERO.
  • 21.4 first target of a double: ZERO / NO BIRD (report), ZERO / ZERO (simultaneous).
  • 21.4 second target of a double: 1st kept, 2nd ZERO.

Loss of self-control

If a shooter shows by his attitude or behaviour that he is losing control, for example throwing his gun or being violent towards a referee or other competitors, the referee must alert the jury as soon as possible. The jury may then exclude him (chapter 23).

  • Throwing a gun, violence: loss of control.
  • Referee alerts the jury at once.
  • Possible exclusion (chapter 23).
More:Conduct
21.6PDF, p. 25 ↗

When you may shoot and load

A shooter may fire only in his turn and only after a target has been thrown. Aiming at other shooters' targets is forbidden, and so is deliberately aiming or shooting at living animals. He may load only on his own station, with the gun pointed down range, after the referee has authorised the start; a semi-automatic may hold no more than two cartridges.

  • Shoot only in your turn and after the target is thrown.
  • No aiming at others' targets or at animals.
  • Load only on your station, muzzle down range, after authorisation.
  • Semi-automatic: max 2 cartridges.
Terms:Safety Conduct
21.6PDF, p. 26 ↗

Keep the gun open: turning round, target viewing, interruptions

The shooter must not turn round on the stand before opening the gun and taking the cartridges, fired or not, out of the chambers. While targets are being shown and during any interruption, the gun must be open and unloaded and may only be closed again with the referee's permission. After a failure he stays in place, gun pointed down range, without opening it or touching the safety until the referee has checked it.

  • Open and unload before turning round.
  • Gun open and empty during target viewing and interruptions.
  • Close only with the referee's permission.
  • After a failure: stay put, gun down range, until inspected.
21.6PDF, p. 26 ↗

No coaching during competition

A competitor may not be coached during competition. If the referee judges that coaching is happening, the competitor gets a yellow card, and repeat offences of the same kind bring the penalties and sanctions of chapter 23. The person doing the coaching is removed from the shooting area by the referee.

  • Coaching during competition is not allowed.
  • First time: yellow card for the competitor.
  • Repeats: chapter 23 sanctions.
  • Coach removed from the shooting area.
21.7PDF, p. 26 ↗

Refusal to comply: 10 seconds to resume

Once the referee has given his final decision, the shooter must resume shooting within 10 seconds. Otherwise his attitude is treated as a refusal to comply and may be sanctioned under chapter 23. Deliberately delaying the shoot or clearly unfair behaviour may also be regarded as refusal to comply.

  • Resume within 10 seconds after the final decision.
  • Otherwise: refusal to comply, chapter 23.
  • Deliberate delays also count.

Common questions on this chapter

How long do I have to call for my target?

At most 10 seconds after the target of the previous station has been shot. The first time you exceed it you get a warning noted on the score sheet; after that the targets are penalised per the 21.5 table.

What happens if my gun misfires?

Stay in place, gun pointed down range, and do not open it or touch the safety until the referee has checked it. The first failure in the round is a NO BIRD with a first warning (yellow card); a second failure of the same type means the targets not shot are scored zero (red card).

How many malfunctions am I allowed per round?

One. The first gun or ammunition incident in a round of 25 targets is repeated as a NO BIRD with a yellow card. A second incident of the same type is not repeated: the targets not shot count as ZERO.

My gun broke in the middle of a round. How long do I have?

Two minutes to repair or replace it. With the referee's agreement you can continue with your group using another gun; otherwise the remaining targets are zero.

I was not ready when the target came. Can I refuse it?

Only if you did not call for it, which is a NO BIRD. Refusing a regular target you called is penalised: a single is ZERO, a report double ZERO with the double repeated for the second, and a simultaneous double ZERO-ZERO.

The referee called NO BIRD but I shot anyway and broke it. Does it count?

No. Shooting at a target after a clearly announced NO BIRD is forbidden and is penalised per the 21.5 table.

What do the yellow and red cards mean in Compak?

A first breach is warned with a yellow card. A further breach on the same Compak gets a red card and the targets are scored per the 21.5 table. Reoffending can lead the jury to take away a target, a whole round, or exclude you.

Can my coach give me advice between stations?

No. Coaching during competition is not allowed. The competitor gets a yellow card, repeats bring chapter 23 sanctions, and the person coaching is removed from the shooting area.

As an interpreter, can I stand next to my shooter on the stand?

The rulebook does not mention interpreters at all. It forbids coaching during competition and removes the person coaching from the shooting area, so keep strictly to translating and agree with the referee in advance where you may stand.

I keep arguing after the referee's final decision. What can happen?

You must resume within 10 seconds of the final decision, otherwise it is a refusal to comply. Continuing to dispute or arguing ostentatiously is an attempt to influence; both can bring chapter 23 sanctions up to exclusion.

When may I load and close my gun on the stand?

Load only on your station after the referee authorises the start, keeping the gun open and the muzzle down range inside the Firing Angle Limiter. Close it only when it is your turn, and unload before turning round or leaving.
