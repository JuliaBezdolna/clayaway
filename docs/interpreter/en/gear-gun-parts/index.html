Parts of the gun, from shoulder to muzzle

Coaches name the parts of the gun all the time, so learn them from back to front. The stock («приклад») goes into the shoulder. Its top edge, where the cheek rests, is the comb («гребінь»); 'raise the comb' or 'drop the comb' is about gun fit. The narrow part held by the trigger hand is the grip («шийка приклада»), and the rubber pad at the end is the recoil pad («затильник»).

The metal centre of the gun is the receiver or action («колодка»). On top sits the top lever: push it aside and the gun opens. Behind it is the safety catch («запобіжник»), often combined with the barrel selector («перемикач стволів», 'selector'), which decides which barrel fires first. Underneath is the trigger («спусковий гачок») in its guard.

A release trigger fires when it is let go, not when it is pressed. Such a gun must carry, as a warning, a sticker with a large 'R' on a fluorescent background on the outer side of the stock.

Under the barrels the other hand holds the fore-end («цівка»). The barrels («стволи») have chambers at the back for the cartridges and muzzles («дула») at the front, where chokes are screwed in. Along the top runs the rib («планка») with a bead («мушка») at the end. The bead is a reference, not a sight: 'look at the target, not the bead'.

Two false friends: rib is not «ребро», and 'break the gun' means open it, not damage it. And one safety rule linked to the parts: after a misfire or any other gun or cartridge failure the shooter must not open the gun or touch the safety until the referee has looked at it.

Key points
  • Stock, comb, grip, recoil pad; receiver with top lever, safety and selector; trigger.
  • Fore-end, barrels with chambers and muzzles, rib with bead.
  • A release trigger needs an 'R' sticker on the stock.
  • Rib ≠ «ребро»; break the gun = open it.
  • After a misfire: do not open the gun or touch the safety before the referee checks.

The coach says

“You're seeing too much rib — your head is up.”
The eye is too high above the rib, usually because the head lifts off the comb.
“Check your selector — you fired the top barrel first.”
The barrel selector is set the wrong way round.
“Break your gun and show me it's clear.”
Open the gun and show empty chambers.
“Forget the bead — look at the target.”
Focus on the target, not on the end of the barrel.

Check yourself

A coach says 'Raise the comb'. Which part does he mean?
  1. The top of the stock, where the cheek rests
  2. The small bead at the end of the rib
  3. The guard around the trigger
  4. The choke tube at the muzzle
Correct!

The top of the stock, where the cheek rests — The comb is the top of the stock; raising it lifts the eye above the rib.

What must a gun with a release trigger carry?
  1. A red-painted barrel to warn others
  2. A large 'R' sticker on the stock
  3. A second, separate safety catch
  4. Nothing: no marking is required
Correct!

A large 'R' sticker on the stock — Both rulebooks require the 'R' warning sticker, on a fluorescent background, on the outer side of the buttstock.

How do you translate 'Break your gun'?
  1. «Зламайте рушницю»
  2. «Розберіть рушницю на частини»
  3. «Відкрийте (переломіть) рушницю»
  4. «Почистіть стволи рушниці»
Correct!

«Відкрийте (переломіть) рушницю» — On a shooting ground 'break' means opening the gun on its hinge so it cannot fire.
