Categories: Junior, Lady, Men, Senior, Veteran, Master

Besides the open ranking, where everyone competes together, FITASC events rank shooters in categories by age and sex. From 1 January 2027 the international table for Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting has six categories: Junior (13–20), Lady (from 13), Men (21–55), Senior (56–65), Veteran (66–72) and Master (from 73, men and women). For events before that date, check the table then in force on the FITASC website.

What counts is the year of birth, not the birthday. A shooter moves into a new category on 1 January of the year in which he or she reaches the age. For the 2027 season this means: Junior born 2007–2014, Men born 1972–2006, Senior 1962–1971, Veteran 1955–1961, Master born before 1955. The limits move on by one year every season.

The biggest interpreter trap is “Senior”. In everyday English it sounds like “adult”, but in FITASC Senior means the 56–65 age group; the main adult category is Men. Do not translate it as «дорослі». “Master” is another trap: the FITASC age category from 73 has nothing to do with the NSCA “Master class”, which is a skill level.

Teams have their own rules: usually three shooters, but the Men team in Sporting™ has four. A junior placed in the Men team, or a junior girl in the Lady team, must also enter that category individually. National federations and clubs may use their own categories, so always check the competition programme, and in shoot-offs remember that categories have their own top three.

Key points
  • Six FITASC categories from 2027: Junior, Lady, Men, Senior, Veteran, Master
  • Year of birth decides; the new category starts on 1 January
  • Senior = 56–65, not “adults”; the main adult category is Men
  • FITASC Master (73+) ≠ NSCA Master class
  • Teams of 3; Men team in Sporting™ = 4; national programmes may differ

The coach says

“Which category are you shooting in?”
Asked at registration: Junior, Lady, Men, Senior, Veteran or Master.
“He’s a Senior now.”
He is in the 56–65 group this season.
“She won the Lady category and was fourth overall.”
First among ladies, fourth in the open ranking.
“There’s a shoot-off for Veteran bronze.”
Two or more Veterans are tied for third in their category.

Check yourself

A shooter born in 1971 enters a FITASC competition in 2027. Which category?
  1. Men
  2. Senior
  3. Veteran
  4. Master
Correct!

Senior — He turns 56 in 2027, so he is Senior from 1 January, whatever his birthday.

How should you translate the FITASC category “Senior” for a client?
  1. “Adults”
  2. “Experienced shooters”
  3. “Top class”
  4. “The 56–65 age group”
Correct!

“The 56–65 age group” — In FITASC Senior is an age group of 56–65; the main adult category is Men.

How many shooters are in a Men team in Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™?
  1. Four
  2. Three
  3. Six
  4. Five
Correct!

Four — Teams usually have three shooters, but the Men team in Sporting™ has four.
