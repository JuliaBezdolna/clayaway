Protection, vest and dress code

Hearing protection is compulsory for everyone on or near a layout: shooters, referees, staff and spectators, and that includes you. Earplugs («беруші») and ear muffs («навушники») both work; electronic muffs amplify voices and cut the bang, which helps you hear the coach and referee. A shooter who comes to the stand without protection is treated as absent.

Safety glasses («захисні окуляри») are compulsory too, for shooters, referees and staff, and in Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ for anyone close to a stand. Lenses come in colours (yellow, orange, purple, smoke) to help see targets in different light.

The shooting vest («жилет») holds cartridges in two front pockets and has a shoulder pad. For Sporting™ it carries a contrasting horizontal line 25 cm below the shoulder axis: before calling, the top of the stock must stay below it, and the referee checks this. Compak® Sporting has no such line because the gun position at the call is free.

Dress rules are the same in both disciplines: no short shorts (only long shorts ending no more than 5 cm above the knee), tops with at least short sleeves, no vest over a bare chest, and no sandals, for safety. The competitor number is worn on the back, fully visible. A breach first brings a warning, then further penalties.

Many shooters also wear a cap with a peak against sun and rain, gloves, and carry a cartridge bag for the day's supply. These are custom, not rules.

Key points
  • Ear protection is compulsory for everyone near a layout, interpreters included.
  • Safety glasses are compulsory; without protection a shooter is treated as absent.
  • Sporting™ vest: contrasting line 25 cm below the shoulder axis; Compak® has none.
  • Dress: shorts no higher than 5 cm above the knee, sleeves, no bare chest under the vest, no sandals.
  • Competitor number on the back, fully visible.

The coach says

“Ears and eyes on before you walk up.”
Put on hearing protection and glasses before approaching the stand.
“Butt under the tape until you see the target.”
Sporting™ gun-down rule: the stock stays below the vest line until the target appears.
“No marker needed here — it's Compak.”
Compak® has no vest-line requirement.

  1. No, only the shooters on the stand

Where is the vest line in Sporting™?
  1. 25 cm below the shoulder axis
  2. At the waist, on the belt line
  3. On the sleeve of the shooting arm
  4. There is none in Sporting™
Correct!

25 cm below the shoulder axis — The contrasting line is 25 cm below and parallel to the shoulder axis; the stock must stay below it before the target appears.

Which footwear is banned in both disciplines?
  1. Trail shoes
  2. Sandals
  3. Boots
  4. Trainers
Correct!

Sandals — Sandals are forbidden for safety reasons.
