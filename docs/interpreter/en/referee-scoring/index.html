Score cards, results and ties

Every target ends up on a score card. In Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ the referee keeps the card, or someone the referee supervises. The FITASC model shows the trap letter of every target, with X for a hit and O for a miss, a total and a signature column. Before the squad moves on, the referee photographs the card.

Shooters must check their score when leaving each stand. A disagreement goes to the referee at once; the referee decides, possibly after asking others, and after that there is no appeal. In Compak® Sporting the shooter signs the score sheet at the end of the round, and a shooter who refuses to sign loses the right to complain. Translate “check your card” as an urgent instruction, not a polite remark.

An international event is 200 targets in rounds of 25. When shooters tie for the first three places, they shoot a shoot-off of 25 targets; if they are still level, sudden death follows, where the first zero eliminates. Below third place there is no shoot-off: tied shooters share the place and are ordered by countback, from the last (eighth) layout or Compak® back to the first. Teams never shoot off.

Shoot-offs bring one more interpreter trap. If no time was fixed, the shooters must be ready within fifteen minutes of the call. A missing shooter is “disqualified” in the Sporting™ rules and “considered withdrawn” in the Compak® rules. Use the word of the discipline you are at.

Key points
  • X = hit, O = miss on FITASC score cards
  • Sporting™: check your score when leaving each stand; Compak®: sign the sheet or lose the right to complain
  • Ties for places 1–3: 25-target shoot-off, then sudden death
  • Below 3rd: countback from the 8th layout or Compak®; no team shoot-offs
  • Ready within 15 minutes of a shoot-off call

The coach says

“Check your card before you leave the stand.”
Look at your marks now; later objections are not accepted.
“Sign here, please.”
Compak®: signing confirms the score; refusing means losing the right to complain.
“Twenty-three — you dropped two.”
Score 23 of 25; two targets were lost.
“There’s a tie for third — shoot-off in fifteen minutes.”
Be at the named layout and ready to shoot within fifteen minutes.
“Sudden death: first zero is out.”
After the 25-target shoot-off, the first shooter to miss (with an equal number of targets shot) is eliminated.
