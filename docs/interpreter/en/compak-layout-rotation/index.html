Layout, menu and rotation

A Compak® Sporting layout is compact by design. The targets must fly over a marked rectangle 35 to 40 m wide and 25 m deep, with a flag or marker at each corner. Facing it are five shooting stations, each a 1 m square, in a line 4 to 8 m behind the front side; station 3 sits in the middle.

There are six traps, lettered A to F from left to right, each with a sign. Every Compak® must include a left-to-right crosser, a right-to-left crosser and a going-away target; the other trajectories are the organiser's choice. Each station has a firing angle limiter, a frame that stops the gun swinging too far and keeps the shooter inside the station.

A round is 25 targets, five at each station: five singles, three singles and a double, or one single and two doubles. On one Compak® the double type is the same at all stations. The menu, the order of targets, is posted at every station, and the first target of each double is the last target shot at the previous station.

Rotation in a squad of up to six: station 1 always starts, and the sixth shooter waits behind it. When the shooter on station 5 finishes, he goes to wait behind station 1, everyone moves one station to the right, and the waiting shooter steps onto station 1. Stations are changed with the gun open and unloaded, without disturbing anyone still shooting.

In-line Compak® works differently: each shooter of a group goes from station 1 to station 5.

Key points
  • Rectangle 35–40 m × 25 m; five 1 m stations; six traps A–F.
  • Compulsory: left-to-right crosser, right-to-left crosser, going-away target.
  • Round = 25 targets, 5 per station; one double type per Compak®.
  • Each double starts with the last target of the previous station.
  • After station 5: wait behind station 1; everyone moves one station right.

Check yourself

How many traps and stations does a Compak® Sporting layout have?
  1. 4 traps, 3 stations
  2. 6 traps, 5 stations
  3. 5 traps, 5 stations
  4. 8 traps, 6 stations
Correct!

6 traps, 5 stations — A Compak® uses six traps, A to F, and five shooting stations.

The shooter on station 5 has finished his menu. Where does he go?
  1. To station 1
  2. He stays on station 5
  3. Behind station 1, to wait
  4. To the referee to sign the sheet
Correct!

Behind station 1, to wait — He becomes the waiting shooter behind station 1 while everyone else moves one station to the right.

On a Compak®, the first target of each double is…
  1. any target the shooter chooses
  2. always trap A
  3. a target shown only on request
  4. the last target shot at the previous station
Correct!

the last target shot at the previous station — The menu chains the stations: each double begins with the target that ended the station before.
