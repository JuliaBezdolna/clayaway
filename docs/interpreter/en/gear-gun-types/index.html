Types of shotgun and what the rules allow

Every gun you will see on the stand is a smooth-bore shotgun. On almost every competition stand it is an over-and-under (O/U, in Ukrainian «вертикалка»): two barrels one above the other, opened by 'breaking' the gun on its hinge. Each barrel can carry its own choke, which matters for doubles.

A side-by-side («горизонталка») has two barrels next to each other. It is the classic game gun and is allowed, but rarely seen in competition. Do not translate over-and-under simply as «двостволка»: that word covers both types.

A semi-automatic («напівавтомат») has one barrel and reloads itself after each shot, throwing the empty case to the side. FITASC allows it, with conditions: no more than two cartridges in the gun, the breech open whenever it is carried, and in Compak® Sporting a device that stops ejected cases from hitting the neighbour.

Pump-action guns, reloaded by sliding the fore-end by hand, are not allowed in Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ or in Compak® Sporting. The rulebooks simply state the ban without giving a reason, so do not invent one when a client asks; just say it is the rule. Compak® Sporting also bans drillings, guns that combine shotgun and rifle barrels.

Two more limits apply in both disciplines: the gauge may not be larger than 12 (so 20 gauge is fine), and barrels must be at least 66 cm long. Slings, gun-mounted cameras and any artificial aiming device are forbidden.

Key points
  • Most competitors shoot an over-and-under («вертикалка»).
  • Semi-automatics are allowed: at most two cartridges, breech open when carried, shell catcher in Compak® Sporting.
  • Pump-action guns are banned in both disciplines; the rules give no reason.
  • Gauge no larger than 12, barrels at least 66 cm, no slings or aiming devices.

The coach says

“Bring your over-and-under and a spare set of chokes.”
The two-barrel competition gun plus extra choke tubes.
“With a semi-auto, keep the bolt open until you're on the stand.”
Safety rule: a semi-automatic is carried with the breech open.
“No pumps on this ground.”
Pump-action guns are not accepted.

Check yourself

Which gun is NOT allowed in FITASC Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting?
  1. A 20-gauge over-and-under
  2. A side-by-side
  3. A pump-action
  4. A semi-automatic with two cartridges
Correct!

A pump-action — Pump-action guns are banned; the others are allowed if they meet the gauge and barrel limits.

How many cartridges may a semi-automatic hold?
  1. One
  2. Two
  3. Three
  4. As many as the magazine takes
Correct!

Two — Both rulebooks limit a semi-automatic to two cartridges.

A client asks why pumps are banned. What is the honest answer?
  1. The rules state the ban without explaining it
  2. The rulebook says they are too slow for doubles
  3. The rulebook says they are less safe
  4. They are in fact allowed in Compak® Sporting
Correct!

The rules state the ban without explaining it — The rulebooks give no reason; as an interpreter, report the rule and do not invent explanations.
