A Sporting™ course: layouts, stands and traps

In Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ the ground is divided into layouts (in Ukrainian «майданчик»). A layout is a group of shooting stands that together give 25 targets: 15 singles and 5 doubles. Depending on the system, a layout has three, four or five stands, each with its own traps.

A shooting stand is where the shooter stands: a 1 m square or a circle 1 m across, level and not slippery. Careful with the Ukrainian word «стенд»: it usually means the whole sport or the shooting ground, so translate stand as «позиція». The rest of the squad waits nearby, close enough to follow but never distracting the shooter.

Traps are placed to suit the terrain, often partly hidden by bushes or trees, so that targets look like real game: crossing, incoming, going away, quartering, high or low, in the open or through trees. The traps of a stand are marked with letters A, B, C, D, read from left to right as seen from the stand.

In front of each stand there is a target menu: it lists the traps in shooting order and shows special targets, such as a 90 mm, 70 mm or 60 mm target, a battue or a rabbit. The shooter reads it before stepping onto the stand, and you should too.

Trajectories are graded by difficulty. Class A is broken by an average shooter more than 80% of the time, class B 60 to 80%, class C 40 to 60%. So a class C target is meant to be hard; a miss there is no disaster.

Key points
  • A layout gives 25 targets: 15 singles and 5 doubles.
  • A stand is a 1 m square or 1 m circle; translate stand as «позиція», not «стенд».
  • Traps are lettered A, B, C, D from left to right as seen from the stand.
  • The target menu shows the order of traps and any special targets.
  • Difficulty classes: A over 80%, B 60–80%, C 40–60%.

The coach says

“Layout three is the hard one today — lots of minis in the trees.”
On the third layout there are many small targets flying through trees.
“On this stand, read the menu before you step in.”
Check the order of traps and special targets first.
“Stand two is a class C target, don't worry about it.”
The target is graded as hard; a miss is expected.

Check yourself

How many targets does one Sporting™ layout give?
  1. 10
  2. 20
  3. 25
  4. 50
Correct!

25 — Every layout type gives 25 targets: 15 singles and 5 doubles.

A coach says 'Go to stand two.' How do you translate 'stand'?
  1. «стенд» (the shooting ground)
  2. «позиція»
  3. «трибуна»
  4. «стійка для рушниць»
Correct!

«позиція» — In Sporting™ a stand is the 1 m shooting position; «стенд» in Ukrainian usually means the sport or the whole ground.

How are the traps of a Sporting™ stand identified?
  1. By numbers 1 to 6, counted from the right
  2. By colours painted on each machine
  3. By letters A, B, C, D from left to right
  4. By the name of the person releasing them
Correct!

By letters A, B, C, D from left to right — The letters A, B, C, D are read from left to right as seen from the stand, and the menu and scorecard use them.
