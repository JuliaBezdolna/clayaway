Viewing the targets

Before a squad shoots a stand, the referee has the targets shown. The presentation is for reading the targets: line, speed, distance and what the clay does. All trajectories are shown to the first shooter of the squad, who must be standing ready on the stand.

The shooter on the stand may ask the referee to repeat the presentation, up to two targets for each target of the menu sequence.

Doubles on report are not shown, because the second target only leaves on the sound of the shot. Simultaneous and rafale doubles are shown to the first shooter of each squad and, on request, repeated up to two shown doubles.

During the presentation guns are open and unloaded, as at every interruption, and they are closed again only when the referee allows it. Shooting or mock shooting during the presentation is forbidden and is penalised like a gun-position offence: a warning first, then a red card.

If a trap breaks down or shooting stops for more than ten minutes, the referee shows that stand's targets again. Before the event nobody may practise on the competition layouts.

In training, viewing is the coach's favourite moment: 'watch where it turns'. In competition the presentation is part of the event, so the coaching ban applies here as well: stay quiet unless the referee or the shooter needs a procedural translation.

Key points
  • All trajectories are shown to the first shooter, standing ready on the stand.
  • Repeats on request: up to two per target of the menu.
  • Doubles on report are never shown; simultaneous and rafale doubles are.
  • Guns open during the presentation; no firing or mock shooting.
  • Interruption over 10 minutes: targets shown again.

The coach says

“Show me the targets, please.”
Shooter to referee: start the presentation.
“Again, please, B only.”
Request to repeat one target of the menu.
“Guns open during the show.”
Referee: open and unload until he allows closing.
“Watch where it turns.”
Coach in training: notice the point where the clay changes direction or shape.

Check yourself

Which doubles are NOT shown at the presentation?
  1. Simultaneous
  2. Rafale
  3. On report
  4. None: all are shown
Correct!

On report — The second target of a report pair leaves only on the shot, so these doubles are not presented.

How many repeats of a target may the shooter on the stand ask for?
  1. None
  2. Up to two for each target of the menu
  3. As many as needed
  4. One per stand
Correct!

Up to two for each target of the menu — On request the referee repeats the presentation, up to two targets for each target in the menu sequence.

During the presentation a shooter closes his gun and swings along a target's line. What applies?
  1. It is forbidden: the gun-position penalties apply, a warning and then a red card
  2. Nothing: viewing is free
  3. The target is shown again
  4. Only the coach is warned
Correct!

It is forbidden: the gun-position penalties apply, a warning and then a red card — Guns stay open during the presentation, and firing or mock firing is penalised under the warning and red-card rules.
