A whole training session, step by step

Before the session, meet the coach for five minutes. Agree on a pause sign, ask which method the coach teaches and which words he uses for lead, and learn his hand signals. Then the group gathers for the safety briefing: interpret it consecutively, sentence by sentence, firmly and completely.

On the first stand the coach usually starts without cartridges: an eye-dominance check, then stance and the ready position. In Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ the gun starts low, below the line on the vest; in Compak® Sporting the gun position at the call is free. Whisper his corrections from behind while he moves feet and hands.

Next comes reading the target. The coach shows it, asks where the shooter first sees it clearly, and sets the pick-up point, the hold point and the break point. Keep these three terms consistent all day, «точка підхоплення», «точка утримання», «точка пострілу», so the guest learns them.

Now the shooting. Between targets the coach gives two- to five-word cues; you mirror them, same length, same order, while the gun is open. When a miss is diagnosed, “behind”, “over the top”, “you stopped”, translate the diagnosis and the fix together. Praise is translated with the same energy.

Then the pairs: which target first, “eyes first, then the gun”, the second shot. Finally the debrief, consecutive again, where the coach sums up two or three points. Write them down in both languages and give the guest the note. Below is a real-world chain of phrases in the order you will hear them.

Key points
  • Before: pause sign, method, lead words, hand signals
  • Safety briefing: consecutive, firm, complete
  • Same three terms all day: pick-up, hold, break point
  • Cues between targets: same length, gun open; diagnosis + fix together
  • Debrief: write the key points in both languages

The coach says

“Morning, everyone. Before we load anything, a quick safety briefing.”
Start of the session; interpret the briefing consecutively and completely.
“Guns stay open until you’re on the stand and I say “load”.”
No closed guns off the stand; loading only on the coach’s word.
“Point at my nose, both eyes open. Now close your left eye.”
Eye-dominance check: if the finger jumps, the right eye is not the master eye.
“Front foot pointing at the break point, weight forward.”
Stance: set the feet for where the target will be broken; lean slightly forward.
“Gun down, stock under the line. Mount only when you see it.”
Sporting™ ready position: the gun comes up only after the target appears; in Compak® Sporting the position at the call is free.
“Watch this one. Where do you first see it clearly?”
The coach is setting the pick-up point.
“Hold your muzzles about halfway back, just under the line.”
Hold point between pick-up and break point, slightly below the target’s path.
“Behind it. Give it more.”
The shot passed behind the target: more lead.
“Call it three feet — that’s the picture you want.”
The lead picture to see at the shot; feet as an impression, keep the unit.
“Over the top. You lifted your head — stay in the gun.”
Miss above, caused by the cheek leaving the stock.
“Smoked it! Same again.”
Perfect hit; repeat exactly what you did.
“You stopped the gun. Keep it moving through the shot.”
Miss behind because the swing stopped; follow through.
“Next is a report pair: crosser first, then the rabbit.”
The rabbit is released by the sound of the shot at the crosser.
“Eyes first, then the gun.”
On the second target, find it with the eyes before moving the muzzles.
“Keep the muzzles under the rabbit.”
Stay low on a rolling target so a hop still meets the shot.
“Change to quarter and half for this stand.”
Fit 1/4 and 1/2 chokes (open, then a little tighter).
“Let’s call it a day. Three things for tomorrow: feet, head, follow-through.”
End of session; the coach sums up — write these down for the guest.

Check yourself

When is the safety briefing interpreted, and how?
  1. Briefly in chuchotage while guests load
  2. At the start, consecutively, sentence by sentence and completely
  3. Only if a guest asks
  4. After the first stand
Correct!

At the start, consecutively, sentence by sentence and completely — Safety comes first and must not be shortened; consecutive mode lets every rule land.

The coach says “Behind it. Give it more.” What is the best short rendering between targets?
  1. «Ви трохи запізнилися, спробуйте ще раз, можливо, швидше»
  2. «Позаду. Більший винос.»
  3. «Стріляйте раніше»
  4. «Добре, так само»
Correct!

«Позаду. Більший винос.» — Mirror the coach: same length, same order, diagnosis plus fix, nothing added.

Why keep “pick-up point”, “hold point” and “break point” in the same Ukrainian words all day?
  1. The rules require it
  2. So the coach can understand Ukrainian
  3. So the guest learns three stable concepts and does not confuse them
  4. It does not matter
Correct!

So the guest learns three stable concepts and does not confuse them — Consistent terms build the guest’s mental map of a stand; changing words makes three concepts look like six.
