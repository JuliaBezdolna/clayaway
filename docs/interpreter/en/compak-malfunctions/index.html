Malfunctions in Compak®

In Compak® Sporting, as in Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™, gun and cartridge failures are counted per round of 25 targets: the first incident brings a No Bird and a yellow card; with a second incident of the same type, the targets not shot at are scored zero, with a red card.

What the shooter must do: stay in place, gun pointed down range, without opening it or touching the safety catch until the referee has inspected it. Opening it first costs the target or targets: zero.

First incident, when both barrels fire together or the first cartridge fails: a single is thrown again, a double on report or a simultaneous double is thrown again, and nothing is scored from the failed attempt. When the second cartridge fails: on a single the target is thrown again, but only the second shot can score, so the first must be a deliberate miss; on a report pair the first result stands and the double is repeated for the second; on a simultaneous double, a new double.

After a malfunction the shooter has two minutes to repair or replace the gun. As an exception he may borrow a gun from a shooter outside his own squad, with the owner's and the referee's agreement. Without a working gun, the remaining targets are zero. Otherwise no gun, choke or barrel may be changed within a round.

The same logic covers a rabbit missed with the first shot that breaks before the second: No Bird, and on the repeat only the second shot counts.

Key points
  • First incident in a round: No Bird + yellow card; second of the same type: zero + red card.
  • Do not open the gun or touch the safety before the referee's inspection.
  • Second-cartridge failure on a single: on the repeat only the second shot counts.
  • Two minutes to repair or replace; borrow only from outside your squad, with owner's and referee's consent.

Check yourself

First incident of the round in Compak®: the first cartridge fails on a single. The shooter waits correctly. Decision?
  1. No Bird with a yellow card; a new target is thrown
  2. Zero
  3. Red card and zero
  4. One
Correct!

No Bird with a yellow card; a new target is thrown — The first gun or cartridge incident in a round is a No Bird with a first warning shown by a yellow card.

A shooter's gun breaks down. He wants to borrow a squad-mate's gun. Is it allowed?
  1. Yes, with the owner's consent
  2. Yes, if the referee agrees
  3. Yes, without conditions
  4. No: the lender must be outside his squad, and both owner and referee must agree
Correct!

No: the lender must be outside his squad, and both owner and referee must agree — Compak® forbids sharing a gun within a group; exceptional borrowing is only from outside it, with both agreements.

Second gun failure of the same type in this round, on a single the shooter could not shoot at. Decision?
  1. No Bird again
  2. No Bird and a warning
  3. Zero, with a red card
  4. The target is shown again
Correct!

Zero, with a red card — From the second incident of the same type, targets not shot at are scored zero.
