Gun

Over-and-under

Also: O/U, Over/under, Superposed

What it is

A shotgun with two barrels, one above the other; the most common type in Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting.

In detail

An over-and-under has two barrels one above the other, a break-open action and usually a single trigger with a barrel selector. A narrow sighting plane along the rib, good balance and reliability have made it the standard in Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting. The two barrels can carry different chokes, so the first and second shots suit different distances. An open over-and-under shows everyone at once that it is safe, which is handy on the stand. It is the most common choice of first gun for a beginner.
