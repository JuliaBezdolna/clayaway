Shooting vest

Also: Skeet vest, Shooting gilet

What it is

A shooter's vest with cartridge pockets and a pad for the gun stock.

In detail

A vest is the first piece of gear worth buying after eye and ear protection. It has two large cartridge pockets, pockets for empty cases and chokes, and a smooth shoulder pad where the stock sits. A good vest does not hinder the mount or restrict movement. In Sporting™ the vest or jacket must carry a contrasting line 25 cm below the shoulder axis for checking the ready position. The FITASC rules forbid wearing a vest with nothing underneath, and the referee may take one cartridge from each of the right and left pockets for checking.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

A contrasting line 25 cm below the shoulder axis is compulsory.

Compak® Sporting

The Compak rules require no vest line.
