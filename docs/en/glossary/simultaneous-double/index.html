Sequences & doubles

Simultaneous double

Also: Simultaneous pair, Sim pair, True pair

What it is

A double in which both targets are released at the same moment.

In detail

Both targets leave together on one call and may be shot in either order. In Sporting they come from one or two traps; in Compak always from two different traps. Simultaneous doubles are shown during the presentation. Not firing at one without a valid reason, or a red-card penalty, scores zero and zero. In Compak, if one cartridge breaks both targets, the double is a No Bird and is repeated the first and second time, and is scored Zero-Zero the third time.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

One or two traps; shown to the first shooter of the squad, up to 2 repeat showings.

Compak® Sporting

Always two different traps; shoot-offs use a Compak with 1 single and 2 simultaneous doubles per station.
