European Firearms Pass

Also: EFP, European Firearm Pass

What it is

A document an EU country issues to resident gun owners for travelling with their guns to other EU countries; valid for up to five years.

In detail

The European Firearms Pass (EFP) is issued by an authority of the EU country where the gun owner lives and is usually valid for up to five years. Specific guns are entered in it, so before travelling check that the gun you are taking is listed and that the pass will not expire on the way. Sport shooters with an EFP and proof of the reason for the trip, most often an invitation letter from the organiser, can usually take a gun to another EU country without a separate prior permit. Countries may still have their own exceptions, and the EFP does not override local rules on carrying, storage and buying cartridges. It is not issued to residents of non-EU countries; check the current rules of destination and transit countries.
