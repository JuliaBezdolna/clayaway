Technique

Master eye

Also: Dominant eye, Leading eye

What it is

The dominant eye, which should look along the gun's rib.

In detail

In clay shooting you usually shoot with both eyes open, and the master eye takes the place of a sight. If the master eye matches the shoulder you mount on (right eye, right shoulder), things are simple. If not, the brain switches to the other eye and the gun shoots to the side. That is why a coach checks the master eye at the first lesson. Glasses with a small dot on the lens or closing one eye help when dominance is partial or crossed.
