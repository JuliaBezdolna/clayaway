Refereeing

Referee

Also: Stand referee, Field referee, Assistant referee

What it is

The official at the stand who judges every target and keeps order.

In detail

At an international competition every referee promises to apply the rules honestly and impartially, to ignore their own nationality and federation, to stand where they can see the targets clearly, to announce decisions loudly and clearly and to carry the current rules in the FITASC official languages. The referee alone decides whether a target was hit or missed, whether a trajectory is correct and whether a target is a No Bird; these decisions cannot be taken to the jury. When in doubt the referee may consult another referee, the trapper or the shooters, but is not bound by their views. Each referee needs a valid national certificate; someone without it may act only as an assistant referee with the jury's prior approval.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

Relays the call to the trapper by audio signal; keeps the scorecard or has it kept.

Compak® Sporting

Must be approved by the jury beforehand; announces every zero aloud or by sound signal; does not look for a missing shooter.
