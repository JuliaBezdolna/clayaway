Head lift

Also: Lifting the head, Head off the stock

What it is

A mistake in which the shooter lifts the cheek off the stock and shoots over the target.

In detail

When the cheek leaves the comb, the eye ends up higher above the rib than it should and the gun shoots over the target. Most often the head comes up because the shooter wants to see the target or the result better. Fatigue, a poorly fitting gun or fear of recoil can also cause it. It helps to keep the cheek on the stock deliberately until the end of the follow-through and to check gun fit. This mistake often appears at the end of a long day, so fitness matters too.
