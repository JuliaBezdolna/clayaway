Organisation

Group (in line)

Also: In-line group

What it is

In Compak in line, the shooters assigned to one Compak: total entries divided by the number of Compaks.

In detail

In in-line Compak the size of a group is the total number of shooters divided by the number of Compaks in use. Every shooter of the group starts on station 1 and finishes on station 5. A gun may be borrowed only from a shooter outside one's own group, and several shooters of the same group may not share a gun. A late shooter whose appeal is accepted may shoot the round with another group.
