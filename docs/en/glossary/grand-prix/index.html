    Discipline

    Grand Prix

    Also: GP

    On this page:What it isIn disciplinesRulesLessonsRelated

    What it is

    A level of Sporting competition for which a special 3-position line with 5 traps per position is allowed.

    In detail

    In the Sporting rules the term matters mainly for the layout: a line with 3 shooting positions and 5 traps per position is allowed for a Grand Prix only. All other layout types still give the usual 25 targets, 15 singles and 5 doubles. Other conditions of a Grand Prix are set by the competition programme.

    In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

    Sporting™

    A 3-position × 5-trap line is allowed for Grand Prix only.
