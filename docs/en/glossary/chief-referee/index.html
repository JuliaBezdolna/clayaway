Refereeing

Chief referee

Also: Head referee

What it is

The senior referee of a competition, holding an international FITASC qualification.

In detail

In Sporting the chief referee needs an international certificate issued by FITASC, takes part in validating the layouts with the jury members responsible for them and then briefs every stand referee. The jury decides technical problems the chief referee has not settled. In Compak the Head Referee must hold an international card and be qualified as Head Referee by FITASC; he may interrupt shooting for a sudden downpour or storm, and an emergency jury decision by two members needs his agreement.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

Called chief referee; validates layouts with the jury.

Compak® Sporting

Called Head Referee; stops shooting for bad weather.
