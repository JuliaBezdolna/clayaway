Round

Also: Series, Round of 25

What it is

A unit of 25 targets shot on one layout, line or Compak.

In detail

In both rulebooks a round is 25 targets. An international competition is 200 targets, i.e. 8 rounds. Gun and ammunition malfunctions are counted per round: the first gives a No Bird, later ones in the same round give zero. In Compak a round is five targets at each of the five stations; gun, choke or barrel may not be changed within a round, and trajectories and menus change only after a complete round. A jury penalty can be the loss of a whole 25-target round.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

Gun, choke or barrel may be changed between stands or between singles and doubles.

Compak® Sporting

No change of gun, choke or barrel within a round (except after a malfunction).
