    Scoring

    Score sheet

    Also: Scorecard, Score card

    What it is

    The official record of each shooter's results in a round.

    In detail

    In Sporting the referee keeps the scorecard, or has it kept under supervision, using a pencil of a different colour for each stand; before the squad moves on the referee photographs the card and marks an asterisk by the name of the shooter carrying it. Shooters check their score when leaving each stand, and a disagreement must be raised with the referee at once. In Compak the referee keeps the sheet when shooting by squad or in line by hand; with electronic systems results appear on a screen. A hit is X and a miss O. The Compak shooter must sign the sheet at the end of each round; refusing to sign removes the right to complain.

    In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

    Sporting™

    FITASC model in the rulebook (p. 19); each round is posted on a central scoreboard.

    Compak® Sporting

    Models in Annex 1 (by squad) and Annex 2 (in line); signature compulsory.
