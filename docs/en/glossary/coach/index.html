Training

Coach

Also: Instructor, Trainer

What it is

A trainer who teaches a shooter technique, safety and competition preparation.

In detail

A coach builds safe habits from the first lesson, checks the master eye, sets up the stance and mount, helps choose a gun and cartridges, and plans the training. Mistakes are much easier to see from outside, so even experienced shooters work with a coach. In FITASC competitions coaching a competitor during the shooting is forbidden: the competitor gets a yellow card, and the referee sends the person coaching out of the shooting area. The ClayArena coach directory helps you find a coach by discipline, and ClayAway tours (in preparation) will let you train with a coach while travelling. Check a coach's qualifications and references.
