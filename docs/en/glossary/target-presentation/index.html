Sequences & doubles

Target presentation

Also: Presentation, Showing targets, Viewing

What it is

Showing the targets of a stand or station to the shooters before they shoot.

In detail

In Sporting™ all trajectories of a stand are shown to the first shooter of the squad, who must stand ready on the stand; on request each target may be shown again up to 2 times. Doubles on report are not shown; simultaneous and rafale doubles are, with up to 2 repeat showings. Nobody may fire or mock-shoot while targets are shown, and after a trap breakdown or a stop of more than 10 minutes the stand's targets are shown again. In Compak® Sporting shot by squad, the day's first squad sees the targets once, early in the morning: singles A to F, then the simultaneous doubles of stations 1 to 5; after a break of more than 10 minutes the six singles are shown again. In line, each new shooter on station 1 sees the opening target of their double. In both disciplines guns stay open and unloaded during the presentation and are closed only with the referee's permission. In Compak® Sporting mock shooting is not allowed away from the shooting positions or while another squad member is shooting.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

Shown at each stand to the first shooter of the squad; up to 2 repeats per target; no firing or mock shooting while targets are shown.

Compak® Sporting

By squad: once, early in the morning, for the day's first squad (singles A–F, then simultaneous doubles); after a stop of over 10 minutes the 6 singles are shown again.
