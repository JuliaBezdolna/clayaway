Biodegradable target

Also: Eco target, Pitch-free target

What it is

A pitch-free target that breaks down more easily in nature.

In detail

Classic targets are made from limestone or another filler mixed with a binder, traditionally pitch, so their fragments lie on the ground for a long time. Biodegradable targets are made without pitch, using other binders, and their fragments break down over time. Many grounds are switching to them to protect the soil, water and land around the ground. They may fly and break a little differently, so on an unfamiliar ground watch the presentation. Composition and properties depend on the maker.
