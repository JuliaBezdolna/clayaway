Target menu

Also: Menu, Firing menu, Order of shooting

What it is

The board at a stand that shows which traps are shot and in what order.

In detail

In Sporting the menu stands in front of each stand where the shooter can read it, lists the traps in shooting order (for example A, B, C) and must mark special targets: 90 mm, 70 mm, 60 mm, battue or rabbit. Referees check before the competition that the machines are loaded to match it. In Compak the firing menu is posted legibly at each station; singles may use the machines in any order, and the first target of each double is the last target of the previous station. FITASC advises the 40 ready-made tables of Annex 3.
