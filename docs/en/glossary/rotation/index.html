    Sequences & doubles

    Rotation

    Also: Shooting order, Changeover

    On this page:What it isIn disciplinesRulesLessonsRelated

    What it is

    The changing order in which squad members shoot at each stand or move between stations.

    In detail

    In Sporting squads of six are formed by draw and the shooting order rotates at each stand and also for the doubles. In Compak by squad station 1 always starts the series; when the shooter on station 5 finishes, he goes to wait behind station 1, everyone moves one station to the right and the waiting shooter steps onto station 1. Stations are changed with the gun open and unloaded, and a shooter has at most 10 seconds to call after the previous station's target.

    In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

    Sporting™

    Order rotates at each stand and for the doubles.

    Compak® Sporting

    Everyone moves one station to the right; the waiting shooter stands behind station 1.
