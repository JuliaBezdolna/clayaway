Ground & layout

Layout

Also: Parcours, Course

What it is

A set of shooting stands and traps that gives one round of 25 targets.

In detail

In Sporting a layout is a group of shooting stands giving 25 targets: 15 singles and 5 doubles. The old system uses 5 traps for 3 positions; new-system lines have 4 or 5 positions. Layouts for national events are approved by the national federation, for international events by FITASC; nobody may practise on them before the event. In Compak the word covers the trap settings and menus of a Compak; trajectories, traps and menus may be changed only after a complete round has been shot by everyone.

In Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting

Sporting™

25 targets per layout; 8 layouts in a 200-target event; no practice on them beforehand.

Compak® Sporting

Set before the start with the ground manager present; changed only after a complete round (50 targets on 2 installations or 100 on 4).
