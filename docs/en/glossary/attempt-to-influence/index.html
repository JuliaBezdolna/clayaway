Penalties

Attempt to influence

Also: Influencing the referee, Pressuring the referee

What it is

Trying to change a referee's decision by pressure, argument or public criticism.

In detail

Three situations count: the shooter keeps disputing a final ruling by words or attitude after shooting has resumed; the shooter challenges or argues about the decision conspicuously; or other shooters (in Compak also spectators) openly show opinions or criticism of a ruling without the referee's permission. The jury may punish it with loss of a target, loss of a round or expulsion, including immediate expulsion on a referee's report.
