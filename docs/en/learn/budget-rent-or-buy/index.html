What it costs to start · 2/4 · 5 min

The gun: rent, new or used

For the first months most beginners are perfectly well served by the ground's rental gun. You learn safety and technique, try different models and find out what suits you. Buying a gun before the first lesson is a common mistake: you may buy a model that does not fit you.

It is time to think about your own gun when you train regularly, know which discipline you will shoot and your coach has seen how you mount the gun. Your own gun, fitted to you (gun fit), helps you shoot more consistently because it comes up the same way every time.

Before buying, check the rules. For Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting, FITASC rules allow a smooth-bore hunting gun of no more than 12 gauge with barrels of at least 66 cm; a semi-automatic is allowed, a pump-action is not. So even a cheap pump gun is no good for these competitions.

New or used? A new gun has a warranty and a known history. A used one can cost noticeably less, and a quality gun that has been looked after lasts for decades. But buying a used gun blind is risky.

Before buying a used gun, show it to a gunsmith. They will check the barrels (dents, bulges, internal corrosion), how tightly the action locks, how the triggers and ejectors work, and the state of the chokes and stock. In many countries guns undergo compulsory testing at official proof houses and are stamped with proof marks; the gunsmith will tell you whether the marks are in order.

Buy only through legal channels: from a licensed dealer or with the transfer of ownership properly registered, as the law of your country requires. A gun without papers or of unknown origin is not a saving but a serious problem.

Remember the extra costs that come with the gun: a slip or case, a cleaning kit, spare chokes, a safe and possibly a stock fitting.

Key points
  • Start with the ground's rental gun
  • Buy your own once you train regularly and have had a fitting
  • FITASC: up to 12 gauge, barrels from 66 cm, no pump-action guns
  • A used gun only after a gunsmith's check
  • Buy only through legal channels

Did you know? A good over-and-under, properly cared for, can serve several generations of shooters, which is why a quality used gun is a common first choice.
