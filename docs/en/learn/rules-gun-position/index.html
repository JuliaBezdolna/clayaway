Rule 1. Gun position before the target

The first thing a newcomer notices at a Sporting™ competition (official name Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™) is that shooters do not hold the gun at the shoulder when they call for the target. It is not a style, it is a rule, called the ready position.

In Sporting™, before calling, both feet are inside the shooting stand and the heel of the stock touches the body. The highest rear point of the stock must stay below a horizontal line on the vest. The shooter holds this position until the target has been released and can be seen.

That jacket line is a strip in a contrasting colour on the vest or jacket, 25 cm below the axis of the shoulder and parallel to it. The referee must be able to see it. The rule applies to every stock: standard, Monte Carlo or adjustable comb.

In Sporting™ the gun may not be mounted before the target appears. Every target, including the rabbit that rolls along the ground, is shot with the gun at the shoulder, never from the hip.

There is one relaxation: in a double, between the first and the second target, you may hold the gun as you like. Before the first target of the double the low position is still required.

Compak® Sporting is simpler: the gun position at the call is free. You may have the stock at your shoulder in advance or hold the gun lower. There is no line on the vest, but the shot is still fired with the gun shouldered.

In Sporting™ a breach first brings a verbal warning with a yellow card, and a repeat on the same layout brings a red card and a lost target. The penalties lesson explains the cards in detail.

The full wording of every item (our summary of the FITASC rules with a link to the official document) is in the ClayArena rules guide, and you can test yourself in the ClayArena rules trainer.

Key points
  • Sporting™: until the target appears the stock stays below the vest line, heel of the stock touching the body.
  • The vest line is 25 cm below the shoulder axis, in a contrasting colour.
  • Sporting™: between the two targets of a double the gun position is free.
  • Compak® Sporting: the position at the call is free and there is no vest line.
  • In both disciplines every shot is fired with the gun shouldered.

Did you know? The low-gun rule keeps Sporting™ close to hunting: a hunter does not wait for a bird with the gun already at the shoulder either.
