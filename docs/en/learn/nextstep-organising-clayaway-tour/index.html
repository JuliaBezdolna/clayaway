How to organise a ClayAway tour

ClayAway is ClayArena's line of organised training tours. A coach or organiser gathers a group of shooters, and each participant buys one tour package: training at shooting grounds, accommodation, transfers, a shared programme and, where needed, an interpreter.

The organiser and the coach are not necessarily the same person. The organiser may be the coach, but also a manager, a club, a shooting ground or an agency. It is the organiser who is responsible for the tour and receives the payouts.

Where to start? Decide on the idea and the name. A ClayAway tour is usually named after the coach or the place plus the format, for example «Cyprus Shooting & Food Week». Then choose the coach, one or more grounds, the dates, the group size and the disciplines — Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™, Compak® Sporting or both.

Next, put the package together: a hotel, transfers from the airport and to the grounds, shared dinners or excursions. If participants speak different languages, bring in an interpreter who understands shooting terminology. The programme timetable is given in the venue's time zone, with the zone labelled.

Do not forget the law. Rules on carrying guns and ammunition differ between countries: participants may need, for example, a European Firearms Pass or an invitation letter from the ground. Help the group sort out documents in advance, but each shooter is responsible for acting legally.

Payment for the tour goes through ClayArena. The participant pays for the package, and ClayArena automatically keeps its commission, a percentage of the amount the organiser receives. Everyone on the tour has a role — organiser, co-organiser, coach, staff, participant, companion — and each sees only the information it needs.

The best tour is one after which participants shoot better and want to come again. So describe the programme, the price and the level the tour is aimed at honestly.

Key points
  • ClayAway is ClayArena's line of training tours: one package for the shooter
  • The organiser may be a coach, club, ground or agency; the organiser receives the payouts
  • The package: training, accommodation, transfers, programme, an interpreter if needed
  • The timetable is in the venue's time zone
  • Gun and ammunition laws differ between countries

Did you know? Every ClayAway tour carries the caption «ClayAway» in the catalogue and on its card, and the tour name follows the pattern «coach or place + format».
