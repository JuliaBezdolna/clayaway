The Lady category

Clay shooting is one of the few sports where men and women shoot from the same stands at the same targets. In Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting there are no «ladies'» trajectories or shorter distances: everyone shoots the same programme.

At the same time, competitions have separate category rankings. For women this is the Lady category (Dame in French). Under the FITASC category table in force from 1 January 2027, it is open to women and girls from 1 January of the year they turn 13, and the category has no upper age limit.

Age counts by year of birth, not by birthday: a girl who turns 13 in December can enter the category from January of that year. An authorised exception (derogation) is possible in special cases, but that is for the federation to decide.

Girls aged 13–20 are also of Junior age. For older women FITASC also has the Master category — from the year of the 73rd birthday, for men and women. Which category you enter in such cases is set by the competition rules and programme, so check when you register.

Besides the individual ranking there is a team ranking. A Lady team is usually three shooters: women or junior girls. A junior girl selected for the Lady team must also enter the Lady category individually.

Important: these are international FITASC rules. National federations and clubs may have their own categories and age limits, so always read the programme of the specific competition. And remember: a category result does not stop you competing for the win in the open ranking, where everyone shoots together.

Key points
  • Men and women shoot the same targets and trajectories
  • Lady — from 1 January of the year you turn 13; no upper limit
  • Age counts by year of birth
  • A Lady team is three shooters (women or junior girls)
  • National events may have their own categories — read the programme

Did you know? In clay shooting women often finish high in the open ranking too, competing with men on equal terms.
