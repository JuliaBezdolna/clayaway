Travelling with a coach on a ClayAway tour

Planning a trip yourself is interesting but takes effort. Another way is to go with a coach on a ClayAway tour. This is ClayArena's service where a coach or organiser gathers a group of shooters, and each participant buys one package.

The package usually includes training at the grounds, accommodation, transfers, meals and a shared programme, plus an interpreter if needed. What exactly is included depends on the tour, so read the description carefully before booking: are flights, targets, cartridges and gun hire in the package?

The main advantage is having the coach with you every day. They know the grounds, help with technique (Sporting™ or Compak® Sporting) and explain how to prepare for particular targets. And a group of like-minded people makes the trip more fun.

Times in the tour programme are given in the venue's time zone, with the zone labelled. After the flight check that your phone shows local time, and follow the schedule by that.

Documents for the gun remain your responsibility. The organiser often helps with an invitation letter and advice on local rules, but the gun's owner applies for the permits. If taking a gun is difficult, ask whether one can be hired at the destination.

If the tour is combined with a competition, remember the FITASC rule: a competitor may not be coached during the competition. The coach works with you before and after, and on the stand you shoot on your own.

Key points
  • ClayAway — ClayArena tours with a coach: one package for everything
  • Training, accommodation, transfers, meals and a programme are usually included
  • Check what exactly the package includes before booking
  • Programme times are in the local time zone
  • Gun documents are the owner's responsibility
