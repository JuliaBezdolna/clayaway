Rules 7 and 8. Cards, penalties, appeals and the jury

Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting share a simple principle: by entering a competition you accept the FITASC rules. A first breach of any article brings a warning, usually with a yellow card.

Repeating the same offence on the same layout (in Compak® Sporting, on the same Compak) brings a red card, and the target is scored: a single is zero; a double on report is zero and No Bird; a simultaneous double is zero-zero.

For more serious matters (repeat offences, refusal to comply, trying to influence the referee) the jury may take away one target, a whole 25-target round, or exclude the shooter. Loss of self-control (throwing the gun, abuse, violence) or deliberately shooting at animals can mean immediate exclusion. Entry fees are not refunded after an exclusion.

One more point: a shooter may not be coached during the competition. If the referee sees coaching, the shooter gets a yellow card and the 'coach' is sent out of the shooting area.

Disagree with a ruling? Protest at once on the stand: raise your hand, say 'appeal' or 'protest' and give the reason. Do it before calling again: by calling the next target you accept the previous ruling. The referee may consult another referee, the trapper or the squad, but decides alone.

Hit or miss, and whether a target was a No Bird or irregular, can never go to the jury. Other referee decisions can be appealed in writing with a deposit: in Sporting™ 40% of the entry fee, in Compak® Sporting the amount in force on the day, and only for incidents of the same day. If the jury agrees with the shooter, the deposit is returned.

After the final ruling, resume shooting and stop arguing: disputing after shooting has resumed counts as an attempt to influence the referee. At the end, check your score on the card, and in Compak® Sporting be sure to sign the score sheet for the round.

The full wording of every item (our summary of the FITASC rules with a link to the official document) is in the ClayArena rules guide, and you can test yourself in the ClayArena rules trainer.

Key points
  • A first breach: a warning with a yellow card.
  • A repeat on the same layout: a red card and a zero.
  • Protest at once on the stand, before calling again: hand up and 'protest'.
  • Hit, miss and No Bird cannot go to the jury.
  • A jury appeal is in writing with a deposit (Sporting™: 40% of the entry fee).

Did you know? A FITASC exclusion stays on record for five years, and a second one within three years can lead to a lifetime ban.
