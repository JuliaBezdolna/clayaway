What ClayArena is

ClayArena (clayarena.com) is a website that brings together the world of clay shooting in Sporting™ (Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™) and Compak® Sporting. Here you can see who shot and how, where the next competitions are and where to train.

The core of ClayArena is competition results. Each competition has a table with places and the number of targets broken. Shooter profiles are built from these results.

A shooter profile is a person's page with their competitions and the key numbers: accuracy, best score and other indicators. They have a lesson of their own.

Besides results, ClayArena has competition videos and season recaps, a competition calendar, a directory of shooting grounds and a directory of coaches.

For those who want to travel and train with a coach, ClayArena has ClayAway, a line of organised training tours. The last lesson of this module covers it.

ClayArena is also about learning: this Academy, a glossary of terms, and a plain-language guide to the FITASC rules with a trainer.

Key points
  • ClayArena is a website about Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting.
  • Its core is competition results, from which shooter profiles are built.
  • It has videos, season recaps, a calendar, shooting grounds and coaches.
  • ClayAway is ClayArena's line of training tours with a coach.
