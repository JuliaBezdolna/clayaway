Olympic Trap and Skeet, and the road to a world sport

Alongside Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting there are two Olympic clay disciplines, Trap and Skeet. They are governed not by FITASC but by the ISSF. Their rules are different, and a shooter coming from Sporting™ will find a completely different rhythm.

In Trap (also called Olympic trench) targets fly away from the shooter out of a bunker in front, at varying angles. Trap was first held at the 1900 Olympic Games in Paris, then came and went from the programme, and has been a permanent part of it since 1952.

Skeet was invented in the USA in the 1920s by hunters who wanted to practise on targets crossing the field from two towers. The name skeet was chosen in 1926 through a contest announced in magazines. Skeet first appeared at the Olympic Games in 1968 in Mexico City.

Separate women's Trap and Skeet events have been on the Olympic programme since 2000, and mixed team events were added later. For many people the Olympics are their first encounter with clay shooting.

It was not only the Olympics that made the sport international. National federations joined international ones; World and European Championships appeared, along with international grands prix and open competitions that draw shooters from other countries. Sporting is now shot on several continents.

Recent decades added another step: the internet. Competition results, videos and rankings are now online, so a shooter can follow the season worldwide. ClayArena gathers exactly this kind of information for Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting, and ClayAway helps you travel to train in another country.

Key points
  • Trap and Skeet are Olympic disciplines governed by the ISSF
  • Trap was first at the Olympics in 1900, permanently since 1952
  • Skeet appeared in the USA in the 1920s and has been Olympic since 1968
  • Federations, championships and now the internet made the sport international

Did you know? The name skeet was chosen in 1926 through a readers' contest: the organisers offered a prize for the best name for the new game.
