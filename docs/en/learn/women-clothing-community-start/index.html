Women in shooting · 4/4 · 5 min

Clothing, community and first steps

A shooting vest has pockets for cartridges and a shoulder pad where the stock sits. Vests and jackets with a ladies' cut allow for the chest and narrower shoulders: the pad ends up exactly where it should and does not get in the way of the mount. In a «men's» vest the stock may catch on the fabric or seams.

In Parcours de Chasse – Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting competitions a dress code applies to everyone: no short shorts (only Bermudas ending no more than 5 cm above the knee), a shirt or T-shirt with at least short sleeves and a collar or round neck, no vest over bare skin and no sandals. Sleeveless tops are not suitable in competition.

Practical tips: comfortable closed shoes with non-slip soles; a peaked cap against sun and glare; tie your hair back so it does not get between your cheek and the stock. Long earrings and necklaces can also get in the way of the mount.

Community. Many clubs have ladies' days and women's training groups where you can try shooting in a relaxed atmosphere, borrow a gun and talk to experienced women shooters. There are also women coaches, whom some women find easier to work with at the start. Ask at your club.

For inspiration, look to world and continental champions in the Lady category, and to women who fight for top places in the open ranking too. Their performances can often be found on ClayArena: in results, videos and shooter profiles.

How to start: 1) book a taster session with a coach at a ground; 2) shoot the ground's gun with light cartridges; 3) bring safety glasses and ear protection (grounds often lend them); 4) after a few sessions have a gun fitting, and only then think about your own gun. Remember that gun and ammunition laws differ in every country.

Key points
  • A ladies' cut vest puts the shoulder pad in the right place
  • The dress code is the same for all: Bermudas no higher than 5 cm above the knee, sleeves, no sandals
  • Hair tied back and few accessories near the face
  • Look for ladies' days and groups at clubs
  • Start: taster session, the ground's gun, light cartridges, fitting

Did you know? A low ponytail can get caught under the stock, so many women shooters tie their hair higher or tuck it under a cap.
