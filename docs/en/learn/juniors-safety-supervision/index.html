Safety and supervision

For young shooters the safety rules are the same as for adults, but supervision is stricter. On the stand a coach or instructor always stands next to the child, close enough to take hold of the gun at any moment. A child is never left alone with a gun — not at the ground and not at home.

Before the first shot the child must know the core rules: the muzzle always points in a safe direction, the gun is open when you are not shooting, you load only on the stand when told to, and on a stop command (cease fire) you open the gun and wait. A good trick is to ask the child to explain these rules in their own words.

Ear muffs or plugs (hearing protection) and safety glasses are compulsory for everyone who shoots or stands nearby. The FITASC rules for Sporting™ make a special point of it: children at the ground outside the clubhouse must always wear hearing protection. Buy children's sizes: big ear muffs slip and protect badly.

Tiredness is the enemy of safety. When a child is tired, cold or overheated in the sun, attention drops, and then it is better to stop. Plan short sessions with breaks, and bring water, a hat and a snack.

At home the gun and cartridges are stored as the law of your country requires: usually in a locked safe, cartridges separately, and the keys held only by adults. A child has no access to guns without an adult.

Parents at the ground set an example too. Stand behind the stand, do not talk loudly during a shot, do not touch other people's guns and always wear ear and eye protection yourself. Children learn above all from what they see.

Key points
  • A coach is always next to the child on the stand
  • The same safety rules as for adults
  • Children's-size ear and eye protection is a must
  • A tired child means the session is over
  • At home guns and cartridges are locked away as the law requires

Did you know? The noise of a shotgun shot can exceed 140 dB, and long exposure even to much quieter sounds damages hearing. That is why spectators need ear protection too, not just the shooter.
