Coaches, shooting grounds and ClayAway tours

A beginner is best off starting with a coach. ClayArena has a directory of Sporting™ and Compak® Sporting coaches: a coach's page tells you who they are and which disciplines they work with.

The shooting grounds directory helps you find where to shoot: a ground's page shows where it is and the competitions linked to it. Before your first visit, contact the ground and ask whether a beginner can come, and whether gun hire and an instructor are available.

ClayAway is ClayArena's line of organised training tours. A coach or organiser gathers a group, and the shooter buys one package: training at shooting grounds, accommodation, transfers, a shared programme and, where needed, an interpreter.

Tours are named after the coach or the place plus the format, for example '<coach name> Training Week' or 'Shooting & Food Week'. The organiser may be the coach, but also a club, a shooting ground or an agency.

Times in a tour programme are given in the venue's time zone, with the zone shown, so you do not mix up the schedule after a flight.

Before you travel, check the rules for bringing a shotgun and ammunition into the tour country, as they differ everywhere. If you have no gun of your own, ask the organiser whether you can hire one on site.

Key points
  • The coaches directory helps you find a coach to start with.
  • The shooting grounds directory shows where you can shoot.
  • ClayAway is a training tour with a coach as one package.
  • Tour programme times follow the venue's time zone.
  • Check gun import rules for each country.

Did you know? FITASC rules forbid coaching a shooter during a competition, so time with a coach comes before the event, for example on a ClayAway tour.
